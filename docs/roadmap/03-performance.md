# Step 3: Performance (outline)

Begins after steps 1 and 2 produce a playable chart autonomously. Detail to be
written then.

## The task

Add the expressive layer to a playable chart:

- Judgment-line motion: impact dips, rhythmic x/y movement, receiver changes.
- Background-line choreography locked to an identified musical layer.
- Effects with a stated role, anchors and lifecycle.
- Section-specific visual identity and contrast, including deliberate rest.

## What exists

Motion tools in the MCP, the judgment-line bank, cadence guidance in
`docs/chart-design.md`, and recorded rejections (run02 sparse punctuation, run03
constant noisy ensemble) showing what does not work.

## Known hard parts

- Rhythm alignment is necessary but not sufficient: it does not establish that an
  effect suits the passage emotionally.
- Activity is not quality. Coverage statistics did not predict the user's verdict.
- Review needs native motion with audio; silent renders only establish geometry.
- Must not damage readability or which line is receiving notes.
