What the numbers mean

Intensity: energy, local accent, attack strength and section intensity, relative to this song.

Priority: how strongly the selection policy favors this candidate. A soft, clear melody can still be selected.

Confidence: strength of detection evidence, not the probability that the charting decision is right.

Hold: a conservative tonal-ridge proposal; its end is not a new strike.
