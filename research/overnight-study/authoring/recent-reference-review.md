## Resulting requirements

1. Default to one main playing anchor at a time. Treat one-note carriers and empty decorative lines as implementation roles, not additional player axes.
2. Separate rhythm/gesture arrangement from presentation; use explicit physical main-axis projection and preserve source-local/side/line provenance. Neither raw local collapse nor arbitrary world-x projection is universally valid.
3. Plan voice/type/hold ownership per phrase. Equal or lower AT object counts repeatedly show that count inflation is not a reliable quality objective.
4. Preserve sparse intervals, long sustains and motif changes. Audio alignment and phrase-specific evidence must precede original authoring.
5. Keep raw objects, new strikes, active sustains and possible shared gestures separate. The corpus contains legitimate IN/AT patterns that violate naive object thresholds.
6. Type quotas are inappropriate: Verrückt IN has zero flicks; QZKago IN has11; PLEASE AT has only38 drags. Deliberate roles matter more than universal proportions.
7. Check moving hold targets continuously and preview lead-in cues, not only head/tail positions. Hidden technical carriers must be distinguished from absent visible playing anchors.
8. Export checks need timing, geometry, alpha and hold-scroll equivalence; successful JSON serialization does not establish visual/readability equivalence.
