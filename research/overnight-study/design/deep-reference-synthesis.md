This is study output, not an original chart. Nine additional continuous official reference clips were rendered and inspected through timestamped two-second frame sequences together with exact note/event data and hit-axis projections. The clips can be played for temporal inspection. Two-second still sampling alone cannot prove every approach is readable; no human playtest or auditory listening is claimed. Root independently inspected the labelled sheets as a second visual review.

`deep_reference_specs.json` records the selected windows/questions. `deep-reference-projection.json` retains source line, note side, local x, transformed hit position, duration, speed, rotation and opacity. `*-canonical.png` projects hit positions onto the dominant engine line's current axis for arrangement analysis. The dominant engine line is a data heuristic, not automatically the main playing line. White rings flag off-axis/angled-carrier candidates requiring source review; they do **not** declare every carrier a special gimmick. This projection is never exported as a rearranged chart.

## What now constrains the original proposal

- Begin with single-axis rhythm, selected voices, lateral motifs and hand flow; add cosmetic guides later.
- Separate main playing line, technical carriers, and empty decorative lines in metadata and review.
- During a hold, its contact point stays on the main playing locus. Carrier approach motion must settle at head arrival and remain stable through the tail unless an explicitly isolated special passage is later approved.
- Drags are travelling contacts; long-looking bars are not necessarily long-duration holds; stacked objects are not necessarily distinct contacts.
- Strong ATs use different routes to difficulty. No note-kind quotas, fixed note-count target, or universal “more holds/more drags” rule.
- Reuse a recognizable rhythmic grammar while actual music changes its accent, duration and answer. Preserve verified tail-free recovery.
- Any later chart must justify new attacks with official/source timing plus digital audio evidence. Uncertain instrument labels are explicitly provisional.
