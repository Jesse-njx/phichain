**What has and has not been established.** Every primary chart was downloaded, scanned in full numerically, and visually inspected as a static schematic atlas. Full music was digitally cross-checked. The parent, artist and corpus reviewer examined selected deeper renderer passages; the added recent gallery records exact sampling and compatibility limits. None of this asserts that every complete chart was watched in gameplay, that a human has playtested every fingering hypothesis, or that official-quality mastery has been demonstrated by a finished original.

**Important interpretation.** Official chart JSON uses formatVersion3 and numeric types1/2/3/4; PhiChain-native format is distinct. The normal compositional baseline is one main playing locus and one ordinary approach axis at a time. Extra engine lines can carry one note into that locus or provide empty decoration. Opposed/inverted/box presentations are deliberate special passages. A raw line count, raw onset group, or displayed line cannot be counted as an independent finger or playing rail.

**Remaining uncertainties.** Twelve old pre-v3 origins/remakes lack exact-day verification; they predate the recent cutoff. Stardust:RAY IN wiki count1382 differs from the downloaded current source's1392 objects, cause unverified. Atlas snapshots are schematic: chart time excludes offset, fixed1.2s note horizon, simplified alpha, short hold glyph tails, and timeline hold duration is drawn at head x rather than its moving world path. Source-local normalization preserves line-local spacing but cannot automatically infer cross-carrier/common-locus coordinates or a safe hand solution. Digital onsets are timbre-sensitive and may miss weak/sustained attacks. Actual playability and official-quality claims require complete synchronized review and skilled human playtests for the completed provisional original chart.
