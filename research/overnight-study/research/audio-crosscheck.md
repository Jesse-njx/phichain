## Interpreting deviations

The actual per-type distributions and ±30/60/100ms sensitivity are in `audio-crosscheck.json`. Compare those with the source grid and listen before adjusting any offset. The tested offsets are sensitivity probes, not a fitted correction.

Examples: Cycle of Redemption IN570 distinct Tap onsets median4.90ms and92.63% within35ms; its sustain/rest structure still needs listening, not peak matching. Verrückt IN median14.67ms and68.42% within35ms despite strong section-density/RMS r=.911; this is a reminder that timing detectors depend on timbre and fast detail. Chronostasis uses a nonzero0.06s chart offset and has median9.00ms; evaluating it with offset0 would compare the wrong temporal origin.
