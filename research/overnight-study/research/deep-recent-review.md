## Reusable composition lessons

- Connected drag vertices are one gesture vocabulary, not a fresh strike at every object. Exoplanetary IN120–128s,70 Minutes Fighters90–94s and sølips118–124s make this visible.
- Count occupied held paths at the current time, then independent answers; do not count approach rectangles or hold head positions as fingers. Entrance AT80–90s, PLEASE76–84s and 祈AT72–74s provide source witnesses.
- Short held steps change articulation while releasing before later steps; preserve actual tails. KIZUNA116–126s and Incyde148–154s are useful variants.
- A real scoring rest can host substantial line choreography. Entrance IN131.594203→136.811594s has no heads and no overlapping holds; the interior after normal release allowance is free. Gungnir’s dark frame requires its decoded tail check rather than visual inference.
- Strong geometric approach drawings may converge at one locus: inspect transformed hit coordinates. Opposed/sideways sustained regions, however, are genuine special geometry and should remain explicit exceptions.
- Restrained judgment-line movement can still deliver rich arrangement: QZKago IN, Lyrith IN and Gungnir IN use lateral contour, fresh/connected contrasts and chord punctuation. All four note kinds need not occur in every phrase.
