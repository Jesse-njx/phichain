This is geometric classification and excludes stops or negative-speed reversal; it cannot establish how long the approach is visible. Counts include raw Drag objects. The examples have decoded heads in `approach-directions.json`, so they can be replayed without relying on the six arbitrary atlas sample times. Empty lines and active holds must be examined separately.

Horizon Blue and Shadow are user-specified box/Paradigm Reboot presentation references. The static atlas does not reliably sample every box scene. No full box-scene renderer clip has yet been produced for these two charts; box/Paradigm Reboot identification remains user-provided, while the decoded direction examples above are independent geometric evidence. Their normalized arrangement should be understood before studying special presentation; a box-shaped image is not permission to treat ordinary AT design as several independent default input axes.

FULi AUTO BUSTER's source confirms regular/downward and upward judgments in concurrent onset groups. SHOOTER also has angled/rightward categories, which helps distinguish the related songs. Cleyera's strict same-time fresh-head search finds no contrasting directions, but the source still contains upward judgments; concurrent existing holds, off-grid timing and earlier motion can carry the special effect, so absence in this narrow search is not absence of a gimmick.

NO ONE YES MAN's canonical line-local timeline and presented contact timeline diverge visibly in the latter half, especially the wide sustain interval before the final return. This is a concrete case where raw local spacing cannot substitute for the presented contact path. Keeping source carrier identities and their transformations is necessary when normalizing.

## Engine lines are not playing lines

Der Schneid AT has24 engine lines:9 contain multiple objects,5 contain exactly one object,10 contain no objects and have positive-opacity event intervals. Source line0 carries1251 of1555 objects, but 'largest source' is not a proven main-playing-line label. A main judgment locus can temporarily be represented by other carriers.

Concrete carriers: line9 owns one object at56.2162s; lines23/22/21/20 each own one object at129.7297/130.1351/130.5405/130.9459s. Their individual transforms can shape separate trajectories without implying four new persistent playing axes. Lines10–19 have zero note objects, so their visible geometry adds no direct contact targets. The two line0 holds begun143.7838s resolve144.8649s. The final four objects on carriers1–4 arrive149.1892s after4.3243s without fresh heads; each is a Hold of only0.067568s duration and speed80, with localx0. Their transformed heads coincide at screen center, carrier rotations90/180/270/360degrees, and opacity0 at impact. They look like long approach bars but are not long-duration sustains. Four co-located objects do not prove four independent contacts. The transferable composition is a pre-cued, isolated converging flourish with empty-line staging after the regular phrase resolves. See [artist exact ending trace](../design/deep-reference-synthesis.md) and [actual rendered ending clip](../design/der-schneid-ending.mp4).

Cycle of Redemption IN's24 engine lines split into19 multi-object carriers,2 single-object carriers and3 empty potentially visible lines; Retribution IN splits16/7/1. Again, engine count mixes choreography roles. `line-roles.json` records every source line's note count, first/last onset and local-x range, so role assignment can be audited before deriving a common-locus arrangement.

Normalize regular arrangement onto one main playing locus first: preserve time, source-local lateral relationships, rhythmic grouping and sustained finger ownership. Then preserve or deliberately remap carrier motion into that locus. When the original intentionally uses opposed directions or a box, mark that as a special passage with its own reviewed rhythm, hand solution, cue and entry/exit. Blindly merging all source-local x values can collapse notes from separately translated lines into false collisions or falsely separate physically coincident notes.
