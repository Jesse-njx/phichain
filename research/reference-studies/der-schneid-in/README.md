## Diagnosis of run03 and next approach

Run03 generators contain concrete mechanical repetition: C assigns targets by
`count[hand] % 4`; B's background uses fixed8beat sinusoidal waypoint positions
then chooses nearby source onsets. Different labels and large excursions do not
establish music-led phrasing. The controller should have challenged this instead
of accepting movement coverage and step-size measurements as adequate review.

Before another whole-song run, build a phrase account for the target song: the
represented rhythmic layer, the exact player action sentence, its repeat/development,
and the visual focal composition. Design a few contrasting connected excerpts
with their entry and exit and let the user judge the play. In a later authorized
revision, lower the main receiver, preserve its identity through rests, give each
section a deliberate visual cast and geometry, and avoid assigning all decorative
lines an always-on job. Keep requested performance density where it serves the
phrase; density alone is not the objective. No run04 is authorized by this study.
