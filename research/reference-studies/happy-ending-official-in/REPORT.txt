Official Happy ending IN versus the five rejected independent drafts
Read-only study, 2026-11-02

The user rejected all five drafts and explicitly authorized inspecting the target
official IN. This overrides the earlier no-official-IN restriction for this study.
No generated chart has been revised. The original source is unchanged.

Main finding
The drafts often change coordinates while preserving almost the same player task.
The official changes contact grammar, simultaneous relationships, reading direction,
receiving geometry and visual staging. These differences are present even when
the overall note count is similar. Adding more decorative events would not repair
the missing action structure.

1. Simultaneous relationships were mostly removed
Official:301 simultaneous onset groups. Drafts A/B/C/D/E:53/71/37/20/18.
These are raw onset groups, not finger-count or difficulty estimates. They show
that similar total note counts conceal a radically different action distribution.

128..132beats,35.07..36.16s: official pairs at128(+/-420),129(+/-210),
129.5(+/-420),130.5(+/-210),131(+/-420), with modest line rotation.
The player repeatedly opens/closes a two-contact formation. E uses mostly
alternating single targets through this same passage. All five have far fewer
simultaneous groups overall. Avoiding sustained sixteenth doubles was reasonable;
flattening slower purposeful double patterns into single streams was not required.

2. Short Holds articulate repeated press/hold/release, rather than long anchors
Official:190 Holds, of which168 last at most1beat; median duration0.65625beat.
Drafts contain21..46 Holds, generally much longer. Official summed Hold duration
is164.1875beats; E's37 Holds total159.25beats. Similar summed contact time is split
into completely different physical tasks.

72..104beats,19.73..28.49s: official has16 Holds and72 Taps, no Drags. Its initial
Holds at72,73.5,75,76.5 last1beat, alongside intervening half-beat Tap answers.
E has4 Holds,27 Taps and75 Drags in the same region: one6beat companion and many
short tap-to-drag packets. That is a different representation of the passage,
not just a slightly easier version of the official task. The return at520 uses
related short-Hold/Tap relationships rather than one unchanged long lock.

144..152beats,39.45..41.64s: paired half-beat Holds alternate with paired Taps;
the chord shifts among left/center/right formations. At480..487beats,
131.51..133.42s, paired half-beat Holds alternate with fresh paired hits while
the pair translates across the field;487 then changes into a short single burst.
These within-phrase task changes are absent from E's all-Tap version of480..512.
This is not a request to impose a Hold quota: the missing behavior is what matters.

3. A short phrase has a changing action sentence
225..228beats,61.64..62.47s: official begins a right-side Tap followed by three
leftward Drag checkpoints, then a left-side Tap with three rightward checkpoints,
then a four-Tap group. Approximate judgment x:
225:469,225.25:360,225.5:255,225.75:150;
226:-440,226.25:-332,226.5:-227,226.75:-122;
227:210,227.25:-210,227.5:0,227.75:-420.
E's224..248 consists of81 Taps and no other type. A fixed alternating-coordinate
cycle cannot reproduce swipe, release, opposite-side answer and fresh-hit close.

49..51beats,13.42..13.97s: the official three-note groups have clear spatial
sentences: right outer -> center -> right inner; left outer -> center -> left inner.
A plausible alternating fingering follows these landmarks. This is a candidate
reading, not observed or enforced hand use. The drafts' independently incremented
per-hand coordinate cycles often preserve alternation without expressing such
short group-level relationships.

4. Receiving motion changes the task, not only the backdrop
All five drafts have one receiving line with x=0,rotation=0 and repeated y pulses.
The remaining three lines carry no notes. Their variations therefore leave the
entire song on the same receiving surface and approach direction.

184..188beats,50.41..51.51s: official introduces an upper receiving surface around
y+247..270,rotated near180degrees, while the lower receiver stays aroundy-270.
Pairs of short Holds approach/land above, followed by paired Taps below. The two
receiving lines tilt together. The repeated section near384 develops the same
upper/lower relationship. E keeps the comparable contact-response material at
the bottom and adds unrelated diagonal scenery. The screenshots show the actual
opposing approaches, not merely a higher line with no gameplay purpose.

The main official line also tilts and changes pulse character within ordinary
lower-line passages: at72/73.5/75 its y returns to-288 and recovers between them,
while its angle develops from0 toward+6degrees. At128 the smaller rhythmic tilt
has a different contour; at184 both receiving planes move together. The drafts
largely reuse the same vertical impact envelope with changed depth/frequency.
The official source approximates curves using many short linear segments; raw
linear-event count is not a useful measure of visible stiffness.

5. Internal carriers and the visible receiving surface are different concepts
All24 official lines contain notes at some time. Many are intentionally invisible
while transporting visible notes. For example,225..227 uses successive hidden
carriers while judgment heads settle along the familiar lower receiving region.
The final roll at553..558.5 uses many carriers for distinct staged trajectories.
It does not display24 full-opacity judgment targets simultaneously.

Native renderer source confirms that line sprite alpha does not hide child notes.
The first analysis draft incorrectly used carrier alpha to classify note visibility;
this was corrected before reporting findings. evidence.json now distinguishes
on-screen judgment geometry, while judgment-heads.json separately records alpha.
Zero carrier opacity must not be confused with an invisible or unplayable note.

6. No judgments does not necessarily mean an empty visual scene
288..320beats,78.90..87.67s contains no official judgment onsets. At beat300,
the native render nevertheless shows four future Taps already participating in
the visual scene. They belong to carriers14..17 and will be judged at352..355,
about96.44..97.26s. The carriers have opacity0 and speed0 from286.25 until their
respective judgment times; their positions/rotations animate the visible notes.
They are future playable notes, not fake decorative objects. The scene develops
into the later fixed-side repeated-hit entry at352..359.

E's comparable section has eight isolated Drags and no ensemble. All five simplify
this middle into very sparse generic pauses. That discards the official's staged
transition. Quiet acoustic features do not require a motionless screen.

7. More lines are not the conclusion
The official also contains busy crossings. Their density alone is not a quality
certificate. In native sequences, families appear, close into narrow formations,
withdraw or exchange roles with short playable gestures. Compare224..227's change
from sparse flanking lines to a gathered crossing formation, versus the drafts'
repeated long sweeps over unchanged Tap streams. The connection between a specific
gesture and its staging is the useful distinction; arbitrary extra lines are not.

Where the orchestration failed
I treated the AT as an allowed deletion/revoicing template, rather than a timing
reference supporting independent phrase composition. The construction brief
prohibited new onset times, the validator prohibited a Hold longer than the
corresponding AT object's original duration, and the geometry checker demanded a
fully opaque carrier at every contact. Those constraints were stricter than the
user's actual goal. They ruled out many of the mechanisms the official uses.

129 of the official's915 distinct onset times are absent from the provided AT
skeleton. At72 the AT supplies a6beat Hold and rapid Drag packets, while the IN
uses repeated1beat Holds with intervening half-beat Taps. Copying/reducing AT
objects cannot discover this different task structure under the imposed rules.
The five agents independently chose variations of the same limited architecture:
single fixed receiver, long-anchor/alternating-stream grammar and looping decor.
I then accepted structural checks and sparse frame observations as sufficient to
build the entire batch. They were not sufficient evidence of quality.

The comparison does not establish a new automatic quality score or prove that
copying the official would meet every user preference. It identifies concrete
missing behaviors and mistaken constraints. This turn ends with analysis only;
no new chart, automatic loop, or edits to the five native projects.
