WHAT IS COUNTED
Discrete attacks = floor taps + sky taps + floor hold starts. No automatic hold/arc scoring ticks.
Colored arcs are stored separately as starts/continuations, not counted as additional discrete taps.
Trace arcs are visual paths; their arctaps ARE counted. A trace without arctaps is not a playable tap.
Arc continuation is inferred by same color, matching endpoint coordinates and <=1ms time difference.
Raw segment rows are retained, so a future reviewer can reassess chains without losing source data.
Game combo totals (wiki917 /1083) include sustained judgments and are NOT these object/attack totals.

GLOBAL INTERVAL EVIDENCE (DISCRETE TIMES ONLY)
Future has36 adjacent73–74ms intervals; Eternal95. These are global intervals, not forced same-hand speeds.
The previous PhiChain generation contains no corresponding short onset intervals because it only used a half-beat grid.
The earlier inference "the onset detector mostly found eighths, therefore avoid sixteenths" was too restrictive.
These human charts are stronger evidence that the song supports selective sixteenth responses and runs.
They do not establish every instrument/lyric attribution; a listening pass is still needed for that.
